## The theme

Read `src/app/globals.css` before changing anything a reader sees. Two rules
in it are correctness rules, not preferences:

- **No colour is ever set inline.** A colour on the element beats the
  variables a row flip redefines, and strands part of the subtree on the
  wrong ground. Measured at 3.36:1 the one time it happened.
- **The accent belongs to the ground, not to the theme.** Amber on a dark
  ground, ink on a light one. A flip swaps the ground, so it swaps the
  accent. Amber on the light ground measures 1.55:1.

Every value in the site's design enters as a token, never as a class. Use
`className` for layout; never override a component's colour or typography.
