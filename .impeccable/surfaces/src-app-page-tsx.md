## Scope and mode

Persuade. Home page (`src/app/page.tsx`) and its shell: header, footer/contact. The site is one page; there are no detail pages. Visitors: hiring leads and prospective clients, arriving from a resume or LinkedIn.

## Audience, proof, constraints

- Proof is real: the four stack hours, the resume, the Zenkoders rows with figures. Nothing invented.
- Build path: code-led (no image generation available). All artwork is hand-authored SVG, original.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One day on the Karachi coast is the page; scrolling moves the clock from dawn to night and each section is a different hour with its own sky, ground and scenery. The stack hours sit between dawn and dusk: Full-stack at 08:30 morning, Data at 12:00 noon, Cloud and CI/CD at 16:30 golden hour, AI at 17:45 evening. Refuses the monospace terminal portfolio and the template hero with a headshot and three skill pills.

OWN-WORLD: Sand-paper cards (#fbf5e6) with a 2px deep-sea-ink (#10333d) border and a hard 6px offset ink shadow, no blur, no radius above 4px. Section fields shift with the hour: dawn peach, morning pale blue, noon pale aqua, golden-hour amber, evening apricot, dusk plum, night navy. Saffron (#f0b53c) primary button, coral (#e0664a) for the italic accent word, sea teal for links. Bricolage Grotesque bold for headings with one Spectral italic accent word each; Hanken Grotesk body; JetBrains Mono for tiny uppercase chapter labels. Hand-built SVG scenery bands: shoreline and dhow, a fish harbour with boats and gulls, port cranes and skyline, dunes with palms, the Sea View promenade with a ferris wheel, dusk beach with camels, night harbour with lighthouse and stars. A pixel camel mascot.

STORY: A visitor understands in one screen that Ali builds full-stack products with AI inside them. They believe it because each stack hour names the tools he uses and what he uses them for, and About ties his four titles to those hours. They act through the resume link or the contact form at night.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating sand-paper header bar with hard shadow (wordmark left with a coral dot, Full-stack, Data, Cloud, AI, About, Resume). Left column: portrait in an arched window frame under a low sun, with the caption "06:10 Good morning from Karachi". Right column: H1 at about 64px, "I build full-stack products with *AI* inside them.", with "AI" as the italic coral word. Under it one sentence of support, the title line "Software engineer / full-stack and AI product", a status dot line, then the saffron "What I build with" button and a "Say hello" link. Bottom third is the shoreline scene: sea bands, sand, a dhow, the hour cue "08:30 Morning at the fish market" riding the water line. Pixel camel bottom right.

FORM: Illustrated scroll-journey landing, the reference's form translated to an original hour-of-the-day world. Pinned by the user; roll key 67e8148a not applied.
