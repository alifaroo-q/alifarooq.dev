## Scope and mode

Persuade. Home page (`src/app/page.tsx`) and the shell every route shares: header, footer/contact, detail pages for case studies, open source and stack. Visitors: hiring leads and prospective clients, arriving from a resume or LinkedIn.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One day on the Karachi coast is the page; scrolling moves the clock from dawn to night and each section is a different hour with its own sky, ground and scenery. Refuses the monospace terminal portfolio and the template hero with a headshot and three skill pills.

OWN-WORLD: Sand-paper cards (#fbf5e6) with a 2px deep-sea-ink (#10333d) border and a hard 6px offset ink shadow, no blur, no radius above 4px. Section fields shift with the hour: dawn peach, noon pale aqua, golden-hour amber, dusk plum, night navy. Saffron (#f0b53c) primary button, coral (#e0664a) for the italic accent word, sea teal for links. Bricolage Grotesque bold for headings with one Spectral italic accent word each; Hanken Grotesk body; JetBrains Mono for tiny uppercase chapter labels. Hand-built SVG scenery bands: shoreline and dhow, port cranes and skyline, dunes with palms, dusk beach with camels, night harbour with lighthouse and stars. A pixel camel mascot.

STORY: A visitor understands in one screen that Ali builds software end to end and designs for what breaks. They believe it because each work card names its decision and its cost, and they act through the resume button or the contact form at night.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating sand-paper header bar with hard shadow (wordmark left with a coral dot, Work, Open source, About, Resume). Below it the chapter label "01 / An introduction". Left column: portrait in an arched window frame under a low sun, with a short italic caption. Right column: H1 at about 64px with one italic coral phrase, one line of support, a status dot line, then the saffron "Explore my work" button and a "Say hello" link. Bottom third is the shoreline scene: sea bands, sand, a dhow, the scroll cue "A little more about me" riding the water line. Pixel camel bottom right.

FORM: Illustrated scroll-journey landing, the reference's form translated to an original hour-of-the-day world. Pinned by the user; roll key 67e8148a not applied.
